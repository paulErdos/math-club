About Complex Mappings

This interactive application demonstrates conformal mapping of complex functions $f(z)$, where $z = x + iy$.

  • Panning & Zooming: Click & drag on any canvas to pan. Use the mouse scroll wheel for smooth scaling.
  • Line Toggle Chips: Independent controls for horizontal and vertical grid sets. At least one line set remains active at all times.
  • Circle Inversion Mode: Automatically splits the workspace into Domain and Codomain planes with a draggable inversion circle in the domain.
  • Time Parameter ($t$): Functions can incorporate variable $t$ (e.g., $z^2 \cdot \sin(t)$) for dynamic transformations.
