Complex Mapping & Conformal Geometry Guide

Complex analytic functions map the plane onto the plane while preserving angles between intersecting curves (conformal mapping). Notice how the neon green horizontal grid lines always intersect perpendicularly with the neon purple vertical grid lines!

Key Mapping Shapes & Behaviors

  • exp(z): Maps horizontal lines into radial rays and vertical lines into concentric circles.
  • z^2: Doubles angles, wrapping the upper half-plane onto the entire complex plane.
  • 1/z: Swaps the inside and outside of the unit circle while reflecting across the real axis.
  • circle_inv: Inverts points across a custom movable circle center with radius R: c + R^2 / conj(z - c).
  • mobius: Linear fractional transformations that map circles and straight lines exclusively to circles and straight lines.
